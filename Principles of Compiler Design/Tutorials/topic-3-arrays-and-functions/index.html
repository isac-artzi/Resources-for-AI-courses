Class activities

One per class meeting. Ungraded — they exist so that the assignment is not the first time you meet the idea.

Activity 1 — Precedence and Associativity Lab

Six expressions, six trees, then break the precedence table and explain each change by the shape of the tree.

Activity 2 — Arrays Are Address Arithmetic

base + i×w by hand, the two-dimensional generalization, then three different answers to where the base comes from.

Activity 3 — Draw the Activation Record

Predict the frame, compare it with Dragon's general form, check it against the generated assembly, then sabotage the epilogue.

Activity 4 — Trace a Call, Including a Nested One

Caller versus callee, $a0–$a3, $v0, $ra — and why f(1, g(2)) works at all.

Activity 5 — Scope and Shadowing

Predict the output, draw the scope stack, break exitScope, then work out what dynamic scoping would have printed.

Activity 6 — Technical Interview Rehearsal

Eight questions, ten minutes each way, at a whiteboard, out loud.
