Computational · The advantage function

Question 1

In state s a policy assigns probabilities π = (0.5, 0.3, 0.2) to its three actions, and the critic's action values are Q(s, a₁) = 2.0, Q(s, a₂) = 5.0, Q(s, a₃) = −1.0. What is the advantage A(s, a₂)? Round to two decimal places.
Hint · A(s,a) = Q(s,a) − V(s), and under this policy V(s) = Σa π(a|s)Q(s,a). The baseline is the policy-weighted average, not the maximum and not the unweighted mean.

Question 5

Question 8

SAC tunes α by descending the dual objective J(α) = α·(H(π) − H̄), whose gradient is H(π) − H̄. You are training on Pendulum-v1, so the target entropy is the standard heuristic H̄ = −dim(𝒜) = −1.0 nat. The current temperature is α = 0.20, the measured mean policy entropy over the batch is H(π) = 0.40 nats, and the temperature learning rate is λ = 0.05. What is α after one gradient step? Round to three decimal places.
Hint · α ← α − λ·(H(π) − H̄). Watch the sign of the target: it is negative, so the bracket is larger than H(π) alone, not smaller.
