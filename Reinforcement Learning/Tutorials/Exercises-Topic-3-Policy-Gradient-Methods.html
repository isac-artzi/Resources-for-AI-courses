Computational · Effective sample size

Question 6

A batch of four trajectories is reweighted and produces the importance weights ρ = (0.2, 0.4, 1.0, 2.4). Compute the effective sample size ESS = (Σρi)² / Σρi². Give two decimal places.
Hint · The weights sum to 4.0 and their squares sum to 6.96. The answer lies between 1 and 4; it equals 4 only when all four weights are identical.
Computational · Entropy of a discrete policy

Question 7

In some state your policy over four actions is π = (0.5, 0.25, 0.25, 0). Compute the Shannon entropy H(π) = −Σa π(a) log π(a) in nats (natural logarithm). Give three decimal places.
Hint · Terms with π(a) = 0 contribute 0, since x log x → 0 as x → 0. Use ln, not log₂: the answer in bits is 1.5, and this is not it.
