Question 6

A transition (s, a, r, s′) arrives with r = 0. You currently hold Q(s,a) = 0.40 and maxa′ Q(s′,a′) = 0.60. With α = 0.1 and γ = 0.99, what is Q(s,a) after the update? Round to two decimal places.
Hint · Form the TD target r + γ·max Q(s′,·), subtract the current estimate to get the TD error, and move α of the way along it.

Question 7

Hint · With probability ε the agent picks uniformly among all k actions — one of which is the greedy one it would have chosen anyway.
