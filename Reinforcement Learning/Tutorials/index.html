Tutorials — Reinforcement Learning

Self-paced, interactive companions to the course syllabus. Each tutorial derives the theory for one topic and shows how it is implemented in practice — with live, in-browser simulations you can drive yourself. The emphasis is not just on what we compute, but on why we compute it that way and not another. Each topic also has a set of ten self-graded exercises.

How to use these files. Open the tutorial for a topic, read the derivations, then drive each interactive demo until you can predict its behavior before you touch the controls. Every demo computes in real time in your browser — there are no AI APIs, installs, or accounts. The "In practice" section of each tutorial connects the theory to the three-tier (Streamlit + FastAPI + Supabase) product you ship for that topic, and the exercises are the quantities you will have to compute inside it.
Where this course sits. Reinforcement learning is treated here as one continuous argument rather than six separate subjects. Topic 1 states the problem; Topics 2–5 build the four families of solution — planning, policy gradients, actor-critic, and search; Topic 6 shows that the alignment methods behind contemporary language models are the same regularised objective the course has already derived, with a different reference distribution. Every topic ships a deployed product, and no deployed service in this course imports PyTorch.
