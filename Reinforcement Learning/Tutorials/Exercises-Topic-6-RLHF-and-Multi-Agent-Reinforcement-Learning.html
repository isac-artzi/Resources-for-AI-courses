Question 6

GRPO samples six completions for one prompt and a verifier scores them r = {1, 0, 0, 1, 1, 1}. Using the population standard deviation (divide by G, not G − 1), what is the group-normalised advantage Âi = (ri − mean(r)) / std(r) of a completion that scored 1? Give four decimal places.
Hint · The mean is 4/6. The population variance of a binary group is p(1−p) with p = 4/6. Then divide the deviation 1 − 4/6 by the standard deviation.
